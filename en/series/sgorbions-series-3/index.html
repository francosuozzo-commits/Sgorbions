The Sgorbions 3 series is a Topps product.
It starts from the American Garbage Pail Kids series 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15.
The base set consists of 256 stickers.
The stickers measure 5.04 cm x 8.89 cm.
The stickers range from 417 to 672.
